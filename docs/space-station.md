# Space Station map

Space Station (`/Game/Crosshair/Maps/L_SpaceStation`) is a copy of the playable Nuketown, re-themed as an orbital station. It keeps Nuketown's exact layout, so trickshot lines, cover and spawns carry over:

- the two houses and the cul-de-sac;
- breakable windows and collision;
- all 21 match spawn points and the navigation bounds.

It is listed fourth under Maps (after Testing Map, Nuketown and Grid), and it is a match map: Offline Match > Match map now cycles Nuketown, Grid and Space Station.

## How it is built

`Scripts/create_space_station_map.py` builds the map through Unreal Editor APIs. It does not edit Nuketown.

1. It duplicates `L_Nuketown` to `L_SpaceStation`. Rerunning the script regenerates the map from Nuketown, so make lasting edits in the script, not in the generated level.
2. It removes the foliage (weeds, flowers, trees, vines, potted plants) and the foliage actor, along with the sky atmosphere and height fog. There is no air in orbit.
3. It remaps materials by surface family. The replacements are procedural and world-aligned in `/Game/Crosshair/Maps/SpaceStationMaterials`, so they need no UVs on the imported brushes:

   | Nuketown surface | Station material |
   | --- | --- |
   | Grass, sand, stone paths, curbs, concrete tiles | `M_Station_Deck`: light grey deck plates with seams and cyan guide lights every 8 m |
   | Siding, plaster, brick, wood, interior walls and floors | `M_Station_Hull`: white hull panels with seams, tonal variation, and orange and blue service panels |
   | Roofs | `M_Station_Solar`: solar-cell panels |
   | Fences, gutters, poles, pipes, beams and metal fittings | `M_Station_Gunmetal` |
   | Cliff rocks around the edge | `M_Station_Asteroid`: the station sits on an asteroid |

   Glass, doors, curtains, books, flags, vehicles, mannequins and invisible tool brushes keep their original look.
4. It sets up the sky and lighting:
   - The sun is a hard white light with no atmosphere tint, and is tagged `CrosshairCustomLighting` so the shared Earth-sky rig leaves the map alone.
   - `M_Station_Starfield` is an 8 km unlit sky sphere. Its shader draws, per pixel, the stars, a faint galactic band, the sun disc and a planet with oceans, continents, ice caps, clouds and a blue atmosphere halo. The planet is ray-traced analytically, so its limb stays perfectly round at any size, and it is lit from the real sun direction, so it shows a terminator. It sits opposite the sun, so it appears mostly lit.
   - The sky light captures only the distant sky sphere, at a higher intensity so shaded walls stay readable, and the station's point and rect lights turn cool white.
   - A soft, shadowless blue "planet-shine" directional light from the planet's direction fills shaded sides. Deck and gunmetal are only partly metallic, because fully metallic surfaces reflect black space and read as dark.
5. It adds ten masts with twin solar wings and red beacons around the perimeter, outside the fences.
6. It adds the station's own post-process grade: histogram exposure held to EV100 -0.5–2 so shaded views can brighten while the vacuum sky stays black, bloom for beacons and guide lights, deep contrast, and light vignette and grain.

Run it locally with the editor closed:

```
UnrealEditor-Cmd <project> -run=pythonscript -script=<absolute path>\Scripts\create_space_station_map.py -unattended -NullRHI -nosound
```

It prints `CROSSHAIR_SPACE_STATION_OK removed=<n> restyled=<n> spawns=21`. `Scripts/probe_space_station_source.py` is the read-only survey of Nuketown's actors, meshes and materials that the surface families were chosen from.

## Credits and limits

The Nuketown geometry and props are Alexey Shishnin's environment; see [Nuketown integration](nuketown-import.md). The station materials, sky, planet and solar arrays are generated by the project. No external assets or services were used.

Gravity, sound and gameplay rules are unchanged from Nuketown. Vehicles, mannequins and some interior props keep their suburban textures. Low gravity or station sounds could be added later.
